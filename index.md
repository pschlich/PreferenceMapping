Internal Unfolding for a product by subject matrix of liking scores.
External Unfolding for constraining the products to be the first PCs of
the product variables (for instance sensory descriptors).
Restricted-Prod Unfolding for constraining the axes to be linear
combinations of the product variables. Restricted-Subj Unfolding for
constraining the axes to be linear combinations of the subject variables
(for instance demographics). Restricted-Both Unfolding for constraining
axes to be both linear combinations of product and subject variables.
PrefMaX as a PCA of individual optimal levels of product variables by
quadratic regressions weighted by fit qualities.
